## Description

A half-zip built around one seam: it runs straight through the cut in the Cave Bear, so the way the garment is made becomes part of the logo. Zip it up when the wind comes, open it when you warm up.

## Design & Purpose

The Traverse Half-Zip is envisioned as a flexible mid-layer for changing conditions and everyday wear. The half-zip construction adds versatility while maintaining a clean, uncomplicated design.

## Fit & Silhouette

A balanced, slightly relaxed fit is the proposed direction, offering enough room for comfortable movement and layering without appearing overly bulky.

## Materials

The material selection will focus on comfort, shape retention, and versatility. A fabric with enough structure for everyday wear and suitable properties for layering will guide further development.<!-- Always written as vision: "planned", "our vision", "what I'm working on" – never as fact. -->
