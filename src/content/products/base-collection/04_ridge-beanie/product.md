## Description

A minimalist beanie designed to bring warmth and understated character to everyday outfits. Its simple form and versatile styling make it a natural companion to the Cave Wear collection.

## Design & Purpose

The Ridge Beanie is envisioned as an everyday cold-weather essential, equally suited to city streets and outdoor settings. Its design keeps things simple, letting the material and silhouette speak for themselves.

## Fit & Silhouette

A close, comfortable fit with a clean shape is the intended direction. The final knit structure and proportions will be defined during product development.

## Materials

The material selection will focus on warmth, softness, and shape retention. A durable knit that remains comfortable through regular wear will guide the design direction. <!-- Always written as vision: "planned", "our vision", "what I'm working on" – never as fact. -->
