## Description

A lightweight grid fleece hoodie combining functional fabric structure with understated design. Finished with a stitched Cave Wear logo, it's designed to bring together thermal comfort, moisture management, and everyday versatility.

## Design & Purpose

The Field Hoodie is envisioned as a functional everyday layer for cooler days and outdoor activities. Its structured fleece fabric is intended to provide thermal insulation while helping wick moisture away from the body, making it suitable for layering and active use.

## Fit & Silhouette

A relaxed silhouette with considered proportions is the intended direction. The hoodie should feel comfortable on its own while allowing room for layering, balancing outdoor functionality with a clean, understated appearance.

## Materials

The proposed material is breathable grid fleece: a lightweight fabric with a textured structure designed to provide thermal insulation and help move moisture away from the body. The final fabric composition and specifications will be established during product development.<!-- Always written as vision: "planned", "our vision", "what I'm working on" – never as fact. -->
