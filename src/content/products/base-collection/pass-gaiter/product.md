## Description

A neck gaiter made for wind, cold and long days outside – and quiet enough to wear in the city. Pull it over your nose when the weather turns, let it rest around your neck when it doesn't.

## Design & Purpose

The Pass Gaiter is envisioned as the small piece you never leave behind. A longer, curved front drop keeps wind out of your collar, and a drawcord on the top edge lets you close it around your face when it really counts. The branding stays black on black: the wordmark on the top edge and the Cave Bear pressed into the toggle – you notice them up close, they never shout.

## Fit & Silhouette

One size, with a stretch that is intended to fit most heads and necks. Worn high over the nose or low around the neck, the front drop sits flat under a jacket collar. The final length and proportions will be defined during product development.

## Materials

The planned fabric is a soft, stretchy knit that stays comfortable on bare skin and through regular wear. The wordmark is planned as a thin silicone heat transfer, pressed into the fabric so it stretches along instead of cracking. Planned: the toggle is made from recycled plastic, tinted black all the way through – the bear is pressed in, not printed, so it can't wear off. <!-- Always written as vision: "planned", "our vision", "what I'm working on" – never as fact. -->
