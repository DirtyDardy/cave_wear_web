## Description

A considered everyday T-shirt built around clean lines and a versatile silhouette. On the back, the cave bear: half skeleton, half outline. On the chest, the embroidered logo.

## Design & Purpose

The Supporter Tee is envisioned as a piece for the ones who were here first: easy enough to wear every day, with a back print you only find on this collection. It can only be reserved before the launch.

## Fit & Silhouette

The proposed direction is a clean, relaxed silhouette with balanced proportions. Final measurements and fit specifications will be established during product development.

## Materials

The material selection will prioritise comfort, durability, and a suitable balance of structure and softness. The back print is planned as a direct-to-garment print, the logo on the chest as embroidery.<!-- Always written as vision: "planned", "our vision", "what I'm working on" – never as fact. -->
