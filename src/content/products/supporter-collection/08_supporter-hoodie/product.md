## Description

A lightweight grid fleece hoodie with a stitched Cave Wear logo on the chest. On the back, the cave bear: half skeleton, half outline.

## Design & Purpose

The Supporter Hoodie is envisioned as a piece for the ones who were here first: a functional everyday layer for cooler days and outdoor activities, with a back print you only find on this collection. It can only be reserved before the launch.

## Fit & Silhouette

A relaxed silhouette with considered proportions is the intended direction. The hoodie should feel comfortable on its own while allowing room for layering, balancing outdoor functionality with a clean, understated appearance.

## Materials

The proposed material is breathable grid fleece: a lightweight fabric with a textured structure designed to provide thermal insulation and help move moisture away from the body. The back print is planned as a direct-to-garment print, the logo on the chest as embroidery. The final fabric composition and specifications will be established during product development.<!-- Always written as vision: "planned", "our vision", "what I'm working on" – never as fact. -->
